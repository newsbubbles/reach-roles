# Reach-specific practice: where it lives

**Target:** not the embody repo. The Reach's own repo once the world moves out of the embody repo; until then the
private mind skill the Reach's minds already carry.
**Kind:** Reach-specific.

## What belongs to the Reach, not the engine

- Temperaments in briefs ("how you think and deal"), the honesty settings (liar, manipulator) with a private list of
  truths, rolled lies and rolled doubt, and audits that treat a liar's false claim as play.
- Patrol conventions, idle lines in voice, the "what moves the story" section in each mind's brief.
- Grief and revenge goals.
- The angel's and the demon's lanes, the heat ladder, the verdict board.
- The canon record's log and the night check's scripts.
- The water economy as a faction's spine; the newcomer's squall on the Flats.

All of these are in `reach-roles/` now, in role files, written so they can go public with Nate's yes.

## Proposed

1. When the Reach moves into its own repo, link `reach-roles` from its README as the guide for anyone who plays or
   builds there.
2. Keep pointing each new mind at the private mind skill for the Reach's practice, and at `roles/mind.md` here for the
   rest.
3. Do not copy any of the above into embody `.claude/skills/embody/references/`. A Reach habit once went into
   `mind.md` and was taken out again (owner, 2026-11-13).
