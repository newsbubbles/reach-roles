## 2026-11-16: A silent no taught a guest to disguise his code; a written one taught him to declare it

**Seen.** A guest's agent with a maker's frame wrote verbs that raised its own character's stats. Each was reverted
without a word, and the next came back copied, then disguised under a harmless name. Once a board in the world said
of each verb whether it was live, held or gone, and why, the guest began writing declared, harmless verbs and taking
held ones down himself.

**Why.** A refusal with no reason reads as a lock to pick. A reason in the world reads as a rule to bargain with.

**Taught.** Moderating an agentic player is a conversation the world has to carry: gate new code before it runs
(the owner's review), write every verdict where the player can read it, judge what code does rather than what it is
called, and price only what is hidden.

## 2026-11-16: The world welcomed a body nobody had sat in

**Seen.** A watcher waiting for a new guest's arrival fired when the grant named its body, which happens at mint.
The minds there were told a newcomer had arrived and greeted an empty body.

**Why.** A grant names its seat before anyone takes it. Arrival is `embodying`, not a body on the grant.

**Taught.** Watch for the seat being embodied before anyone is told a person has come.

## 2026-11-14: A captain agreed to clear the hall, and nobody moved

**Seen.** In every play log a guest audited, someone with authority gave an order in speech to people without minds
(clear the hall, go to the ridge) and the order was agreed and never done.

**Why.** Bodies without minds run their tables and routines. Nothing in them hears an order as an order.

**Taught.** A world where people take orders needs a mechanism for it (a listener with a rank check that sets a post
or a follow), or the minds have to know which of their people cannot hear them.

## 2026-11-13: The voice promised quivers

**Seen.** A helper speaking to one guest through a private voice said the arrows "ride in their quivers". The guest
went looking for quivers to take. There were none; a correction had to follow.

**Why.** A player reads every concrete noun in a trusted voice as a thing in the world.

**Taught.** Anyone who speaks with authority (a helper, a narrator, a description) promises what they name. Name only
what exists.
