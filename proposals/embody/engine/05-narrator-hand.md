# 05. Narrator: the game master's hand in a hosted world

**Target:** embody `.claude/skills/embody/references/narrator.md`, a new short section at the end.
**Kind:** engine-general.

## The change

```markdown
## In a hosted world, beside the minds

A narrator in a hosted world shares the people with their minds and the players. Keep a hands-off list at the top
of your notes (every body a mind or a player holds) and update it the moment one is taken: a body with a mind is
never voiced, not even through someone else's line. Re-read a body before you stage a scene with it; a stale read
put a returned blade back on a guard. Every improvisation needs state behind it: a cup you call a water source holds
water, a prop you narrate as usable has a verb. Walks take time: a body you move keeps a walking pace, because the
minds track who passes them. Fill a rules gap by hand only until the engine fills it, then withdraw your ruling in
story. Own a miss in the voice of the person who made it. Log every check, "no touch" included, for the next
narrator.
```

## Why

All from the first owner-grant game master's log on the hosted Glass Reach (2026-11-09 to 11-13):

- A courier walked home instantly, ahead of the carrier she followed; a scout's mind saw her pass in silence.
- Cups set up as a water source had no water value, so a scout could not drink at his own well.
- A scene restored a blade from a stale read.
- A ruled dawn recovery for wounds had to be withdrawn when healing verbs shipped.
- When an elder got a mind, the narrator's log recorded a rule change: no more voicing her, even through another.
