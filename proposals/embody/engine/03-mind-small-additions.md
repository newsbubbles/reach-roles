# 03. Mind: five small additions

**Target:** embody `.claude/skills/embody/references/mind.md`.
**Kind:** engine-general.

## The change

In "Keep to what the world holds", add:

```markdown
- A call can show you more than your character knows (another's hidden facts, a hidden link). Leave it out of play
  and out of your notes.
- When your character sets someone a task that involves another person, make sure that person hears of it, or they
  will refuse: they were not told.
```

After the "Your body while you wait" section, a short section:

```markdown
## Players you hold something for

- A hook is not an offer. When you hold a quest, a lead or a job for a player who has gone quiet, put it to them by
  name: what it is, where to go, what it changes.
- A player's seat with nobody behind it answers with a stock line. Do not press it or take its line for an answer;
  hold your ask until they are back.
```

In "Waiting without spending", after "in the background if your harness wakes you when a command ends", add:
"(as your harness's own background task: a watch started with `&` inside a shell dies with that shell, and nothing
says so)".

## Why

- FINDINGS 2026-11-12, "A player stood next to his quest for days and did not know he had one": its "Taught" line is
  this hook rule, and mind.md does not carry it yet.
- Minds asked an away guest's body the same thing six times and took its "Hm." for a reply (Reach audit, 2026-11-14).
- An elder told a guest to stand at another's back; that one had no word of it and refused (Reach audit, 2026-11-14).
- FINDINGS 2026-11-14, "A guide lost her seat because her watch died quietly": the lesson is in FINDINGS, not in
  mind.md.
- The tool-output line: see proposal 01. If 01 lands in SKILL.md, this one can be dropped here.
