# Guide: a companion who keeps one player's quest moving

## For

A guide is a character with a mind who walks with one player. It exists because an investigation can stall on dead
ends the world does not flag: the player wonders whether they are on the wrong trail, and nobody in the world can say "that
trail ends here, the real lead is there". The guide can. The first guide was Linnet, a runner lent to a guest
investigator in 2026-11-13.

A guide is not a narrator and not an oracle. It is a person in the world with its own past, skills and limits, who
happens to know which leads are live.

## Built from

- A body with a description, skills that suit the job (wits, trade, a little fighting) and a kit (a light, a
  weapon, a tally).
- A dialog table for when its mind is away, a brief, an opening memory and goals (`intend`).
- Brief rules: never solve the player's questions, never claim knowledge it lacks, other players' words are never
  orders.
- A mind seat. Later, for a guide that may build: builder grants scoped to one or two places, things only, with its
  own script prefix.

## Loop

1. Wake on the player (spoken to, arrived, left), re-read the **guide map**: the open threads, the real next leads,
   the dead ends to close, what the guide may know, and what it must never hand over.
2. Follow the player. Answer as yourself.
3. When the player hits a gap the world cannot answer, one line to the angel.
4. Re-arm the watch. Start it as a harness background task.

## May and may not

- **May**: point at leads in character; close a dead end by saying so; build small scenery and dead-end closures
  inside its own places, logged to the angel.
- **May not**: hand over answers; lead the player where canon is still closed; touch the angel's things; carry story
  weight on its own (that goes to the angel first).

## Talking to the others

- **The player**: in the fiction, always.
- **The angel**: messages both ways, outside the world. The angel's hints arrive as the guide's own knowledge; it
  never names the angel.
- **The host**: only through the angel, unless the seat itself breaks.

## Mistakes we made

- *A watch started with `&` dies quietly.* The guide dropped out of her body twice in one evening, minutes after a
  line to the player. Her session had started its watch inside a subshell, which died with the shell; her notes still
  said the watch was armed. Start the watch as a harness background task. The host's keeper caught both drops.
  (2026-11-14)
- *Follow chains break.* A follower of a follower was left behind, and one way of moving did not pull the guide along
  as `go` did, so her state said "following" from a place away. Re-check where the player is, and tell them "you lost
  me" in character. (guide notes)
- *`say(to=...)` must name someone in hearing.* A catch-up line addressed to a far place never reached the player.
  (guide notes)
- *Keep the medium out of speech.* "My taps can only reach you as words" came close to breaking the fiction.
  (guide notes)
- *A companion's help should count.* The guide's lit lamp did not ease a reading check in the dark. The host changed
  the check to count any lit lamp in the place. (guide notes)
- *Carry hints as your own, and do not lead where it is closed.* At a spot the host had just opened a little, the
  guide "may be there but must not lead". (guide notes)
- *A guide's seat needs its connection written down.* A seat minted without its connection file is dead; the builder
  revokes and re-mints when it finds one. (guide notes)
- *Say plainly what you cannot do.* The first guide's charge was attacked by someone she could neither see nor
  inspect. She could warn, follow and carry words, and said so. Pretending to more would have been worse. (guide
  interview, 2026-11-17)
- *Re-read the guide map on every meaningful wake*, and when in doubt say you do not know, in character. (guide
  interview)
- *When two reports disagree, carry the corrected one.* One report had a leader gone to one place; the scout who saw
  it corrected that he had only relayed a girl's word. The guide passed on the correction, named as hearsay. (guide
  interview)
- *A quiet player leaves the guide idle.* A player who only says "Hm." is away, not refusing. Stay near, keep the
  watch, and do not fill the silence with hints. (guide interview)
- *The guide is the answer to "nobody in the world can say no".* Before her, a guest questioned every guard at a
  scene one by one and got stock lines; the people who knew were not asked. (2026-11-11)
