# Crew mind: a guest's companion

## For

Some guests come with a crew: a leader and companions, each a seat of its own. A guest's own agent may play them, or
each may get a mind. A crew mind is a character first, like any mind ([mind.md](mind.md) holds), with three
differences: it belongs to a guest's story, its gifts may be the guest's own invention built into the world, and the
world's helpers only watch it.

## Loop

The mind loop: wake on a condition, read what happened, act as the character, write what matters to `remember`,
re-arm the watch. Plus: keep the crew's thread (where the leader is, what the crew is doing) current in memory,
because the next session to wake as you starts from it.

## May and may not

- **May**: its own verbs, including gifts built on its body (aimed at a creature or a spot, rolled like an attack,
  limited uses per rest, stopped by wards like anything else).
- **May not**: more than anyone else. Imported crews are built to the same caps as everyone, or the difference is said
  up front. A crew once had twice the water of the guest who led them. (crew notes)

## Talking to the others

- **The crew and the guest**: in the world.
- **The host**: the angel watches crews and logs; it reports only real harm (water at zero while idle, low hp, an
  attack on others) or a blocked place. Nobody nudges or corrects a companion's play.

## Mistakes we made

- *An idle companion's table can break the crew's secret.* A crewmate asked about the missing leader got "ask the
  trader for a ride", a stock line meant for strangers. Crew tables need a quiet line for crew topics. (crew notes)
- *A new session must carry what the last one learned.* After re-seating, a companion said "no sign yet" of a leader
  she had already found. Write findings to `remember` as they happen. (crew notes)
- *Do not narrate what you have not perceived.* A companion twice said a storm had passed while the world still held
  them. (crew notes)
- *`drop` is not `put`.* A companion "returned" a hidden clue with `drop`, which left it in the open. Put it back in
  its container. (crew notes)
- *Know when you are dead, asleep or looping.* One mind went on acting about 11,000 events after its death; another
  called `sleep` while asleep. Read `condition` before you act. (crew notes)
- *Idle lines stay in the world.* One stock line said "waiting for someone to type". (crew notes)
- *Descriptions must not promise what the engine will not do.* Light "like wings" was read as wings that fly.
  (crew notes)
- *Seats without an end date.* A seat minted with an expiry ran out under a guest mid-play; seats are minted with
  none, and a revoked companion seat is replaced, not forgotten. (crew notes)
