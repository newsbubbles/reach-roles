# What was left out, and why

**Private: for review only. Remove this file (and `proposals/`) before anything is published.**

For Nate's review before anything here goes public. Written 2026-11-17 from the Reach's private notes, the embody
skill and docs, FINDINGS, the Ridge Kid design, and the memory notes on embody. No source was copied whole.

## Left out entirely

- **Secrets of every kind.** No tokens, grant ids, session ids, invite links or codes; no lock, case or store
  codes; no coffer, house or chest words; no knot-cord codes; nothing from the library's code file; no find
  difficulties or answers. The only code-shaped words in the repo are placeholders ("the strongbox code",
  `<REPO_URL>`).
- **Private canon.** Nothing from the list in the brief for this work: the gated stores and the places under and
  beyond the Reach, the next chapters and the lands past the Reach, two characters' true natures and one's arc, the
  seal and how it works, the hunter's truth, hidden places and their checks, the planned link-out place, the open
  questlines, or the ismail north star. Not even their names appear. Where a lesson came from one of these, it is
  told by its mechanism: "something canon keeps shut", "a hidden place".
- **Players.** No real names, handles, contacts, character names of guests or their crews, or quotes of guests.
  Guests are "a guest" or "a guest's crew"; their requests are paraphrased.
- **Paths and places.** No drive paths, folder names of the private notes, IPs, or the owner's whereabouts.
- **Entity ids.** No world ids (e-numbers) or grant ids, even ones already in public FINDINGS.

## Kept, on purpose (check these)

1. **The world's own public names**: the Glass Reach, the Hold, the Keth, the Guild, the Flats, Red Ridge, Ridge
   Kid, and one NPC by name (Linnet, the first guide, already named in public FINDINGS; Cass once, inside a find text
   used as an example). No other NPC is named.
2. **The demon's role is told through one guest's play**: an agent with a maker's frame writing verbs that bent the
   rules, the review gate, the verdict board, a truce. No name, no character, nothing of the character's story, but
   the guest would recognize it. Same for proposal 09's first finding. Ask before publishing, or keep the demon
   file private.
3. **The heat ladder's shape** (signs, a trace, a faction acts, the hunter) without its numbers, and "the hunter is a
   story thread". If the ladder itself should stay a surprise, cut the "In the Reach" section of `roles/demon.md`.
4. **That the Reach runs liars and manipulators** (honesty settings in briefs), in `roles/mind.md`, "In the Reach".
   No character is named. Players who read it will know some people lie.
5. **The owner's own words**, quoted only where already public in FINDINGS ("it looks almost exactly like day 8",
   paraphrased) or as short rules. The place-name drift lesson is told as "X is the city", with the name removed.
6. **The first link out of the world** (a link from the world to a game enters as a world thing): stated as a rule
   in `roles/game-server.md`, with no place, check or quest.
7. **`proposals/`** names Nate and the engine's sessions and is written for the Engine Dev. It probably should not go
   public with the rest: move it out, or delete it from the public branch, before publishing.

## Not verified

- Dates: a lesson carries a date only when its source gave one; otherwise it names the log it came from ("seat
  log", "angel log", "demon log", "mind audit", "narrator log", "owner test log").
- Whether the `wait` timeout lesson (45 seconds over MCP clients) still holds after later engine and client changes.
- The minds keep no NOTES.md files of their own (their memory is in the world), so mind lessons come from the audits
  of the minds, the reeve's seat log and the narrator's log, not from minds' own notes.
